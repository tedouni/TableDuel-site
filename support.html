How do I restore my purchase?
Open Table Duel, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases (it is also on the Full Table purchase screen). Use the same Apple Account that bought it. It needs an internet connection.
How does Family Sharing work?
Full Table supports Family Sharing. If you share purchases in your Apple Family group, family members can use Full Table on their own devices without paying again. Make sure Family Sharing and "Share Purchases" are on in Settings on your iPhone.
What are the free tries?
Every game has 2 free tries. After that, that game is locked until you buy Full Table ($3.99, one time), which unlocks every game forever.
Can I play on a regular iPhone or iPad?
Yes. On any iPhone the games run in split screen: one player uses the top half and the other the bottom half, with the phone laid flat between you. Foldable iPhones that open into a large flat screen give each player more room, but every game works on both. On iPad the table is bigger; players sit at the short ends in landscape or top and bottom in portrait.
Can I play alone?
Yes. Every game has a CPU opponent on Easy, Normal or Hard. Run the Table, a solo score run with a Game Center leaderboard, and Showdown need Full Table.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Playing needs no internet. Only buying or restoring Full Table and Game Center use the network.
How do Game Center achievements work?
If you are signed in to Game Center, Table Duel reports achievements to Apple. In Settings you can open your achievements in Game Center. If you are not signed in, progress is saved on the device and reports later.
Does Table Duel collect data or use analytics?
No. The app has no analytics and sends no usage data. See the Privacy Policy.
Notifications keep interrupting Air Hockey
Turn on Do Not Disturb or a Focus before you play, so banners don't land on the table mid-rally.
