Games for iPhone Duo

Unfold it. It becomes a two-sided table.

Open the iPhone Duo flat on the table and each half faces one player, turned the right way up for them. The hinge is the net, the centre line, the middle of the board. Both halves take touch at the same time, so in the fast games you both play at once, across the fold.

  • Each half faces one player

    Your score, clock and controls are upright on your side. Nobody reads upside down and nobody shares a thumb.

  • Play across the hinge

    Pucks cross it in Air Hockey and Shuffle. Boards sit on both sides of it in Drop Four and Dots and Boxes. Quick Draw turns it into a standoff.

  • Fold it and keep going

    Close the Duo mid-match and the game pauses on the cover screen. Drop Four, Quick Draw, Math Dash, Straight Face and Topple carry on there in classic split screen; the others wait until you open it again.

  • Hold it tall between games

    Pick up the open Duo with the hinge across and the menus stand upright with you, so you go from picking a game to playing without turning the phone.

No. 01 to 08In the box

Eight tables, eight materials.

Every game is learnable in one sentence and over in about a minute. Reflexes, physics, strategy, arithmetic and a bluff: there is a table for whoever is sitting across from you.

  • 01

    Air Hockey

    Drag to hit.

    Smash the puck past your opponent's mallet. First to five goals, or ahead when the 90-second clock runs out.

  • 02

    Quick Draw

    Hold. Wait. Tap!

    Hold your thumb on your pad, wait for the signal, tap the target first. Jump the gun and the point goes across the table.

  • 03

    Drop Four

    Drop. Make four.

    Line up four discs before your opponent does, with a chess clock ticking on every move.

  • 04

    Dots and Boxes

    Close the most boxes.

    Draw a line, close a box, move again. Long chains decide the endgame, so count before you give one away.

  • 05

    Topple

    Tap to drop, don't topple.

    Take turns dropping a sliding block onto one shared tower. Whoever knocks it over loses the round.

  • 06

    Math Dash

    Tap the answer first.

    The same sum appears on both sides, with the answers shuffled so nobody can copy. Fastest correct tap scores.

  • 07

    Straight Face

    Beat the claim or call the bluff.

    Press and hold your own cup to peek at your dice. Claim higher than the last claim, or call it. Three lives each.

  • 08

    Shuffle

    Closest puck scores.

    Pull back, aim, flick. Your pucks glide across the hinge into the rings on the far side, and the next shot can knock them out.

Ways to play

Two players, or one against the table.

  • Head to head

    Two players

    Pick a table, both hold Ready, play best of three (or one or five, in Settings). Local multiplayer on one device: no second phone, no sign-up, no waiting.

  • On your own

    Solo vs CPU

    Every game has a computer opponent, tuned in whole matches so a child can win on Easy and a good player has to work on Hard.

    EasyNormalHard

  • First to three

    Showdown

    Random tables, dealt one after another, two players or versus the CPU. First to three game wins takes the series. Full Table only.

  • Solo leaderboard

    Run the Table

    Three lives, every game in random order, Normal for your first four wins and Hard from then on. Your score goes to a Game Center leaderboard. Full Table only.
