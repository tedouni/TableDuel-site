Privacy Policy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no data collected.

Effective date: 2026-11-08

Table Duel is made by Tayseer Edouni, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the Table Duel iOS app does with information. The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no data collected. Nothing you do in the app is sent to me.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up. I do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • No ads and no third-party advertising SDKs. No tracking across apps or websites, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No analytics. The app does not send usage data to me or to any analytics service.
  • Purchases, Game Center and the rating prompt are handled by Apple.

What is collected and why

Purchases (Apple)

The Full Table in-app purchase uses Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes payment and sends the app a confirmation. I never see your payment details or Apple Account.

Game Center (Apple)

Achievements, and your Run the Table score if you post it to the leaderboard, are reported through Apple's GameKit. If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple handles that data under Apple's privacy policy, and other players can see your Game Center nickname and score on the leaderboard. I do not receive your Game Center identity.

Data stored only on your device

Your player names, your Reduce motion and Dim surfaces choices and other preferences (such as match length and CPU difficulty), your match records and achievements progress, the number of free tries used per game, your local match tally, your Run the Table personal best, and a cached purchase status are stored on your device. I cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them, except the free-tries count, which is kept in your device's Keychain so free tries are not reset by reinstalling. It never leaves your device.

Sharing

I do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or combine it with data from other apps or sites. The only third party involved is Apple (purchases, Game Center, and the App Store).

Retention

I do not collect usage data, so there is nothing for me to retain. Apple keeps purchase and Game Center records under its own policies.

Children's privacy

Table Duel does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It has no accounts, chat, ads, social features or links in gameplay. The app sends no data about you off your device, apart from what Apple handles for purchases and Game Center. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. I do not collect personal data in the app, so there is generally nothing to access or delete. California residents: I do not sell or share personal information, and I do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. To exercise any right or ask a question, email me. Where applicable, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

The app does not send your data to me. Purchases and Game Center use Apple's encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy I will update the effective date above, and for significant changes I will note it in the app update notes.
